Product pitch

Crux

After the loop is built, the question is who may know — and what you send the model.

What?

Not another coding extension.

Crux is the product that sits after harness engineering: enterprise knowledge with an authority, then compression only on what that authority already allowed.

What?

Two modules. One decision order.

Enterprise Ontology

Utopia. Who may read or propose. Two restricted bases. Review is not a live edge.

Token Optimization

Headroom. Compress after authorize. Unpublished. Not a knowledge store.

Why?

The expensive failure is a wrong prediction that already used tools.

  • The agent searched a KB, called a tool, then acted on the wrong fact.
  • A staging bot that sees Production is a governance failure, not a prompt failure.
  • More files in context do not decide who was allowed to know.

Why? · $

Price the wrong action first. Tokens second.

wrong_action ≈ incidents × (rework + compliance + blast radius)

token_bill ≈ tokens × calls × price

A model you fill with your numbers — not a Crux revenue claim. Token savings are a hypothesis. Compressing unauthorized text is worthless.
