VPN error 809 on Windows: enable IKEv2 passthrough
Category: Network / VPN

Symptom: The corporate VPN client fails on Windows with error 809 ('The network connection between your computer and the VPN server could not be established').
Cause: IKEv2 UDP ports 500 and 4500 are blocked by the local firewall or the home router.
Resolution steps:
1. Open Windows Defender Firewall > Advanced settings > Inbound Rules.
2. Allow UDP ports 500 and 4500 for the VPN client executable.
3. On the home router, enable VPN/IPSec passthrough.
4. Retry the connection. If it still fails, switch the client protocol from Automatic to IKEv2 explicitly and retry.
Escalate to NetOps if the failure persists off the corporate network.
