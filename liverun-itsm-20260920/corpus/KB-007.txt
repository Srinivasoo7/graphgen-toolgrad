Requesting software installation (admin approval)
Category: Software / Licensing

Approved software is installed from the Company Portal (self-service).
For software not in the portal:
1. Open a ticket with the software name, version, vendor, and business justification.
2. Your manager approves in the ticket; Security reviews the vendor.
3. Typical turnaround is 3 business days after approvals.
Never install with local admin rights granted for another purpose; misuse is revoked and logged.
