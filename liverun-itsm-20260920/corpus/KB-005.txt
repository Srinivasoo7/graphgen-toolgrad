Outlook not syncing: rebuild the OST
Category: Email / Collaboration

Symptom: Outlook shows 'Disconnected' or new mail never arrives.
Resolution steps:
1. Check the status bar: if it says 'Working Offline', toggle it off under Send/Receive.
2. If still stuck, close Outlook, delete the .ost file under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Outlook, and reopen Outlook to rebuild.
3. For shared mailboxes that stay stale, remove and re-add the mailbox under Account Settings.
Do not delete the mailbox profile itself; the OST rebuild preserves all server-side mail.
