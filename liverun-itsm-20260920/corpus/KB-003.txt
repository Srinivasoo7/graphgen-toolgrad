Printer shows offline: rejoin the follow-me print queue
Category: Facilities / Printing

Symptom: the printer shows offline or jobs sit in the queue.
Resolution steps:
1. Confirm the printer has power and a network link light.
2. On your laptop, remove the old direct printer and add 'FollowMe-Print' from the software center.
3. Badge in at any FollowMe printer to release your jobs.
If jobs still stall, note the printer hostname (sticker on the front) and open a ticket; do not reinstall drivers manually.
