Requesting a new laptop for a new hire
Category: Hardware / Provisioning

Laptop requests for new hires go through the hardware catalog:
1. The hiring manager submits a request at least 10 business days before the start date.
2. Standard issue: 14-inch business laptop, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD.
3. Engineering roles may request the 32 GB RAM configuration with director approval.
4. IT images the machine and ships it to the employee's home or holds it at the front desk.
Track the request with the ticket number issued at submission.
