VPN on macOS: import the Tunnelblick profile
Category: Network / VPN

macOS users connect with Tunnelblick:
1. Install Tunnelblick from the Company Portal.
2. Download your personal .tblk profile from the VPN self-service page (requires MFA).
3. Double-click the profile to import, then Connect from the menu bar icon.
Profiles expire after 365 days; re-download from the same page. Do not share profiles between users.
